# Shadow or law?

When physics measures a number very precisely, is that number fixed by a law of nature, or is it a shadow?
Many measured values depend on a frame, an epoch, a convention, a history or a choice of model: they are projections of an invariant whole.
This paper states that whole-first view, then tests a procedure that asks, before any formula is fitted, which numbers could be fixed by structure.
Applied to 74 entries our own programme had collected, mostly precisely measured constants, it leaves just one conditional law candidate and files even our own gravity formula as a product of history.
The v3 registered reliability test failed (α = 0.0955), with its cause located; a separately preregistered v4 repeat on 60 fresh quantities gave α = 1. The historical arm reached 6/6 positives and 0/5 false positives on recognized cases.
The thesis, the test, its failures and its philosophical limits are presented together.

## Whole, frame, shadow

The shadow principle's established instances are textbook physics: rest mass versus frame-dependent energy and momentum,
the Aharonov–Bohm holonomy versus the local field, the cross-ratio under central projection. In each, the equations stay
unchanged; what turns is which side is primary. The universal form, that what physics measures is a projection of an
invariant whole and the whole is primary, is the paper's thesis (H0), not something the census measures.

One structure, read three ways: the whole is the invariant; the frame is the reference system and metric through which
the whole is read; the shadow is the outcome. This reading is an interpretation, not a derivation; it uses the vocabulary
of a long tradition that called the invariant whole measure (Heraclitus, Pythagorean number, Plato's *Philebus*).

## The procedure and its seven dispositions

A projection is many-to-one, so measured shadows identify the whole only up to its fibre: a precisely measured number can
be fixed by structure only if it is constant across the admissible completions of what is known. The procedure first names
the physical carrier, observable, admissible completions and permitted inputs, with the target excluded from those inputs,
and stops at the first applicable disposition, before fitting a numerical expression:

| Disposition | Meaning within the stated scope |
|---|---|
| Law candidate | A structural target remains; this is not an established mechanism |
| Output of history | Contingent dynamics or free inputs set the measured value |
| Model choice | Admissible completions give different values and no independent selector picks one |
| Not yet a quantity | The proposed expression lacks a physical carrier/readout |
| Convention-bound | A unit or defining convention fixes the reported form |
| Epoch-dependent | A frame or epoch enters the reading |
| Already governed | An established physical result already accounts for it |

A refusal is scoped to the declared map and premises, with a reopening condition.
It is not a theorem forbidding every possible future mechanism.

## Calibrations: v3 failed, v4 tested the repair

**The registered three-family test (card v3) is a FAIL: Krippendorff α = 189/1979 = 0.0955**, below its registered fail boundary of 0.4 (a reliable result needed at least 0.667).
All three families passed 6/6 disguised controls, and the majority matched the sealed reference on 59/60 items from outside our catalogue;
those are different statistics and neither rescues the failed agreement. The failure traces mainly to one family not
applying the card's natural-form step (it stopped at the units on 43 of 44 unit-bearing items), a step the pretest
had flagged as under-specified: an instrument failure as well as a lane failure. The other two families, those of the
instrument's builder and adjudicator, agree at α = 0.927: exploratory, not a pass, and not independent of the
instrument's authors. Earlier rounds gave α = 0.639 and 0.389. The census arm's α = 4467/6847 = 0.65 on 47 non-example
rows is descriptive and has no pass threshold.

A blind reliability test with the same three families failed its registered bar (Krippendorff α=0.0955), mainly because one model family skipped the procedure's then under-specified step of writing a quantity in natural dimensionless form; with that step made mandatory and checkable, a separately preregistered repeat on 60 fresh quantities gave α=1 (identical dispositions on every item; the bar for strong agreement is 0.8). Judged at the knowledge state of their time, all six numbers that a later law fixed—among them a hydrogen line ratio and Kepler's exponent 3/2—were filed law candidates, and all five historical numerical patterns were refused; the annotators recognised the cases, so hindsight may have helped. Since physics advanced whenever two names proved to be one thing, we also state a rule for such translations and offer whole, frame and shadow as a common vocabulary in which the philosophical traditions and physics can describe one structure and check each other.

A matched story grammar gave 13, 87 and 140 selected accounts among 1,614 numerical-hit decoys; the analytic medium
selected-expression rate is 4.99985% of hit-bearing targets, not of all 100,000 draws. The resulting likelihood-ratio
ceiling of about 20 (bar about 50) is conditional on a valid matched null, not demonstrated odds. In a blind lineup all 39
decoys tied the flagship: mid-rank 20.5/40 in each family, retest 6/6. Under the registered rule the story adds no weight.
The route catalogue holds 163 nonempty verdicts in a 180-entry index, a map of documented attempts, not independent
impossibility proofs.

## What produces the shadows: argued, not measured

What produces the shadows is not identified by them, so the view that the universe is blind mechanism arising by chance
is not a result of physics either. The paper argues that the lawful, intelligible order of the shadows is better explained
if their source, the whole itself, is reason-like, the Logos of the old tradition, than if it is chance, which already
presupposes law, or a brute order, which leaves its own intelligibility unexplained. This is philosophy with stated
defeaters; no calibration in the paper measures its truth, and the lineage it cites is context, not proof.
