## Context

Helium-3 nuclear magnetic resonance detects the nuclear spin precession frequency in a calibrated magnetic field. Atomic electronic shielding changes the nuclear response compared with the bare nucleus. CODATA combines frequency and magnetic-moment data; the displayed ratio uses angular frequency rather than cycles per second.
