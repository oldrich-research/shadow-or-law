## Context

Atomic magnetic-response calculations use elementary charge, Bohr length and electron mass to express magnetizability coefficients. CODATA tabulates the SI conversion scale in joules per squared tesla. Magnetic response of a particular atom requires its own coefficient in addition to this scale.
