## Context

The cubic term in an atom's induced dipole expansion is expressed in this scale when atomic units are used. The conversion combines elementary charge e, Bohr length a_0 and Hartree energy E_h. The entry is the scale in SI, not the cubic-response coefficient of a specific atomic species.
