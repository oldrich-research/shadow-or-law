## Context

Nonlinear electric response expands the induced dipole moment in powers of applied electric field. Atomic calculations express the quadratic coefficient in this scale using elementary charge e, Bohr length a_0 and Hartree energy E_h. The CODATA entry tabulates the scale in SI, not the nonlinear response of a particular atom.
