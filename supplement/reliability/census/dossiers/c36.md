```text
name: KSS shear-viscosity to entropy-density ratio
definition: Shear viscosity divided by entropy density, reported in the dimensionless form k_B η/(ℏ s).
dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: 1/(4π)
sigma: no observational sigma for the analytic result
source: Kovtun, Son and Starinets (2005), Physical Review Letters 94 111601; https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0405231; η/s calculation for the specified thermal gauge theories
value_confidence: high
```

## Context

The cited calculation treats strongly coupled thermal gauge theories with a classical gravitational description. A Kubo formula extracts viscosity from a stress-tensor correlator, while thermal entropy provides the denominator. The displayed result has stated theoretical assumptions and is not a measured value for every fluid.
