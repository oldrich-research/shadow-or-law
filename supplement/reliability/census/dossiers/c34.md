## Context

The international SI specification assigns numerical values to seven constants and realizes units through physical experiments. Josephson voltages relate frequency to voltage, and quantum Hall resistance plateaus relate resistance to h/e². The Cs frequency refers to the unperturbed ground-state hyperfine transition; K_cd refers to radiation at 540 THz.
