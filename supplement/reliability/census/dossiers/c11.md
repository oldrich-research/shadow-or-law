## Context

Helium emission lines in low-metallicity ionized regions and deuterium absorption in quasar spectra constrain the first two entries. Lithium uses old metal-poor stars; stellar destruction makes the quoted plateau estimate more appropriately a lower bound on primordial abundance. Nuclear reaction calculations provide a separate predicted abundance package.
