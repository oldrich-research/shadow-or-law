## Context

Scattering form factors and spectroscopic finite-size shifts constrain charge radii. The deuteron contains two nucleons and the alpha nucleus four, while their electric distributions also include binding effects. The quoted radii describe electric charge, not geometrical hard-sphere boundaries.
