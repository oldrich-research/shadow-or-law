# c51 — Baryon to photon number ratio η

```text
name: Baryon to photon number ratio η
definition: Number density of baryons divided by photon number density after electron-positron annihilation.
dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: 6.04e-10 (BBN deuterium inference)
sigma: 1.18e-11 (1σ)
source: Particle Data Group 2024, Big Bang Nucleosynthesis review; https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-bbang-nucleosynthesis.pdf; Eqs. 24.2–24.6: elemental abundances and η_10=6.040±0.118
value_confidence: high
```

## Context

The PDG deuterium-abundance analysis reports η_10=6.040±0.118 for standard big-bang nucleosynthesis with three neutrino species. Nuclear reaction inputs and measured primordial deuterium enter the inference. Microwave anisotropies provide an independent baryon-density estimate; the number densities in η refer to the same time after electron-positron annihilation.
