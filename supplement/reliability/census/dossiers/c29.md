## Context

The NCBI standard translation table lists TAA,TAG and TGA as stop triplets. Synonymous codons can be grouped by amino acid, with methionine and tryptophan having one codon each. Any automorphism comparison must specify the nucleotide graph, its action and the block partition; a translation table alone does not supply an orbit computation.
