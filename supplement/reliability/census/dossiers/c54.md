## Context

A blackbody photon spectrum determines number density through its temperature. Photons alone have s/(k_B n_γ)=2π⁴/[45ζ(3)]; including standard thermally populated neutrinos gives approximately 7.04 after electron-positron annihilation. The entropy budget must name the species; the two values are not interchangeable.
