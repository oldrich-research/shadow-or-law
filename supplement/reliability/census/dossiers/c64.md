## Context

The angle is inferred from a global electroweak fit, and the mass ratio from boson mass determinations. The on-shell expression 1-M_W²/M_Z² is not numerically identical to the MS-bar angle after radiative effects. Neutral-current asymmetries and charged-current measurements provide complementary information.
