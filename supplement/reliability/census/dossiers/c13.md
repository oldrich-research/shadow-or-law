# c13 — MSS quantum-chaos bound

```text
name: MSS quantum-chaos bound
definition: Upper bound for λ_L ℏ/(k_B T) in thermal quantum systems satisfying the stated analyticity and factorization assumptions.
dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: λ_L ℏ/(k_B T) ≤ 2π
sigma: no observational sigma for the analytic bound
source: Maldacena, Shenker and Stanford (2016), JHEP 08 106; https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01409; thermal Lyapunov inequality and its hypotheses
value_confidence: high
```

## Context

An out-of-time-order correlation function measures growth of an initially small disturbance. Its exponential growth rate is λ_L over an intermediate time interval. Temperature sets the inverse thermal time; the inequality supplies an upper limit and does not require every thermal system to attain it.
