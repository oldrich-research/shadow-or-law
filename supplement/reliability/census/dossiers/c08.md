## Context

The microwave anisotropy spectrum constrains the baryon loading and cold dark matter contribution. This evaluation uses Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing, flat ΛCDM and H0=67.36 km/s/Mpc. Massive-neutrino density is a separate contribution from Ω_c.
