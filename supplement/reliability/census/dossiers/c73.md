# c73 — SO(d,2) dimension exponent expression

```text
name: SO(d,2) dimension exponent expression

dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: p(4)=-3; p(5)=0; p(6)=7/2; p(7)=15/2; p(8)=12
sigma: zero for integer group dimensions; α^p inherits the uncertainty of α
source: CODATA 2022 electromagnetic coupling; SO(n) algebra dimension n(n-1)/2; https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/Table/allascii.txt; α entry; integer-dimension expression explicitly supplied as the definition, not an observed particle mass
value_confidence: low
```

## Context

The real orthogonal algebra has dimension (d+2)(d+1)/2. The expression associates each chosen integer d with a power of the low-energy electromagnetic coupling. A particle identification is separate from evaluation of this group-dimension expression.
