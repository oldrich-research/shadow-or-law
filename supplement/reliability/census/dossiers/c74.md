# c74 — Bekenstein entropy-bound coefficient

```text
name: Bekenstein entropy-bound coefficient
definition: Coefficient C in S/k_B ≤ C E R/(ℏ c) for an isolated complete system of energy E and circumscribing radius R.
dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: 2π
sigma: no observational sigma for the analytic bound
source: J. D. Bekenstein (2000), Physical Review A 62 062313; https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0006003; Eq. 1 and discussion of its hypotheses
value_confidence: med
```

## Context

The expression compares a system's entropy to its energy-size product. Its hypotheses concern complete bounded systems and the treatment of gravitational effects. A bound coefficient is not the entropy of every system, and a specified specimen need not saturate the inequality.
