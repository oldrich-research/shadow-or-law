## Context

Precision spectroscopy, Penning-trap mass comparisons and nuclear reaction energies constrain the rest masses. The quoted calculation uses CODATA bare-nucleus rest-energy entries and therefore does not include electronic binding. The entries share adjustment inputs, so full covariance is required for a precision uncertainty on their differences and quotient; the displayed propagation explicitly omits it.
