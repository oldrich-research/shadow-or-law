## Context

Gravitational lensing smooths acoustic peaks and correlates different sky modes. Here A_L is fitted from the TT,TE,EE+lowE power spectra with that multiplier allowed to vary. It is not identical to the amplitude estimated independently from the four-point lensing reconstruction.
