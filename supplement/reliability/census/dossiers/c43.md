## Context

Radiation density decreases faster than nonrelativistic matter density during expansion. Equality sets a characteristic horizon and influences the matter transfer function. The four entries refer to the same Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing fit and are correlated transformations, not separate specimens.
