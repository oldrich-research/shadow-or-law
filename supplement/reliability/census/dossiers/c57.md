```text
name: Primordial non-Gaussianity amplitudes
definition: Bundle local,equilateral and orthogonal f_NL coefficients of primordial bispectrum templates.
dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: local=-0.9; equilateral=-26; orthogonal=-38
sigma: 5.1; 47; 24 respectively (68%, statistical)
source: Planck Collaboration 2018 results IX, Astronomy and Astrophysics 641 A9 (2020); https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.05697; combined temperature and polarization bispectrum estimates in abstract
```

## Context

Three-point correlations of microwave temperature and polarization constrain the templates. Foreground subtraction and estimator validation control contaminants. Each coefficient corresponds to a different triangle dependence in Fourier space; the displayed uncertainties are the combined-temperature-and-polarization Planck results.
