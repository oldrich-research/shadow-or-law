## Context

The adopted fit propagates primordial fluctuation power through linear gravitational growth to today. Weak-lensing and galaxy surveys constrain related amplitude combinations through separate observations. Both quoted entries come from the Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing fit and share Ω_m and fluctuation inputs.
