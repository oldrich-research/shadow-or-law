# c31 — Bekenstein-Hawking entropy coefficient

```text
name: Bekenstein-Hawking entropy coefficient
definition: Coefficient multiplying horizon area A/ℓ_Pl² in black-hole entropy S/k_B.
dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: 1/4
sigma: no observational sigma; analytic semiclassical coefficient
source: S. W. Hawking (1975), Communications in Mathematical Physics 43 199–220; https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02345020; original article, p. 204 entropy-area expression
value_confidence: high
```

## Context

A stationary black hole has a temperature proportional to surface gravity. Combining that temperature with black-hole mechanical relations yields the horizon entropy-area expression. The coefficient here refers to the leading Einstein-gravity term, not corrections from extra interactions or quantum effects at small area.
