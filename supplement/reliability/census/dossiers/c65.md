## Context

KamLAND-Zen reports a xenon-136 half-life limit above 2.3e26 years (90% CL). A light Majorana-neutrino exchange interpretation combines this limit with a nuclear matrix element and phase-space factor. Interference among the three mass terms depends on two Majorana phases that oscillation measurements do not determine; the stated d=6 assignment is an additional input.
