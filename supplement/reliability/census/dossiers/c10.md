## Context

COBE/FIRAS measured the monopole spectrum against an internal calibrator. The cited reanalysis combines temperature determinations with absolute calibration corrections. Adiabatic cosmic expansion changes the radiation temperature according to T(z)=T0(1+z) after thermal decoupling.
