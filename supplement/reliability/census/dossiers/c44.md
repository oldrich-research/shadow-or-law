## Context

Laboratory determinations of G use torsion balances, pendulum methods, or atom interferometry. Electron mass measurements use precision spectroscopy and frequency ratios. The expression (4/3)α^21 exp(-5α/2) is proposed for comparison with the measured coupling at low energy; it is not the measurement itself.
