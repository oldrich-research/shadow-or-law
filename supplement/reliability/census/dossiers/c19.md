value_confidence: high

## Context

Peak spacing in microwave angular spectra constrains the sound-horizon to distance ratio. The quoted result uses Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing. θ_* is the physical angular sound horizon, not the approximate sampling variable θ_MC.
