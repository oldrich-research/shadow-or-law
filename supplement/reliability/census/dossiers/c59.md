## Context

The density uses Ω_Λ times 3H0²c²/(8πG) in SI and is converted to natural units before taking the fourth root. Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing supplies H0 and Ω_Λ. This is an energy-density expression, not the mass of an observed dark-energy particle.
