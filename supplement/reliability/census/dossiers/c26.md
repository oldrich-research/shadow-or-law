## Context

Radiation changes the expansion rate and acoustic structure of the microwave sky. N_eff describes relativistic energy density rather than a direct head count. The three known lepton and quark generations are independently catalogued in particle experiments; a hypothetical extra species need not contribute one full unit to N_eff.
