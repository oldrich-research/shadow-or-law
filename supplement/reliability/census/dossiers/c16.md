## Context

The value shown inverts the PDG running-coupling expression with five active flavours, MS-bar coefficients b0 through b3 and M_Z=91.1880 GeV. It is a stated conversion of the reported coupling, not an additional direct measurement. Changing the perturbative order, integration expression or active-flavour treatment changes the reported integration energy.
