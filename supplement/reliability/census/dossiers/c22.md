## Context

The sound horizon integrates sound propagation in the photon-baryon plasma up to the end of significant drag on baryons. It supplies a characteristic separation for baryon acoustic oscillations. The product uses H0=67.36 km/s/Mpc from the same Planck fit, not an independent distance-ladder result.
