## Context

Long-baseline appearance probabilities compare neutrinos and antineutrinos to constrain the phase. The quoted fit is the normal-ordering result without the additional SK atmospheric likelihood in PDG Table 14.7. A common antiunitary symmetry acting on all lepton terms is an extra premise of the proposed zero invariant, not an oscillation measurement.
