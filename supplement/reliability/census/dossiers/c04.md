## Context

The scalar amplitude sets the initial fluctuation power entering microwave and matter spectra. Reionization suppresses small-angle microwave power by a factor exp(-2τ), creating a fit correlation. The entries are Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing marginal results for two representations of the same amplitude.
