## Context

Large-angle E-mode polarization strongly constrains the integrated scattering probability. The quoted Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing analysis uses a tanh transition in ionization fraction. Different ionization-redshift profiles can produce the same integrated optical depth.
