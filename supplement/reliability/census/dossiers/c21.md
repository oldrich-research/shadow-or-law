# c21 — Z/A-dependent gravitational source coupling

```text
name: Z/A-dependent gravitational source coupling
definition: Long-range coupling in which neutral test-body gravitational charge per unit mass is proportional to nuclear proton count divided by nucleon count; readout is η=2(a_Ti-a_Pt)/(a_Ti+a_Pt).
dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: η(Ti,Pt) = -1.5e-15
sigma: 2.3e-15 statistical; 1.5e-15 systematic
source: Touboul et al. (2022), Physical Review Letters 129 121102; https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15487; abstract and final differential acceleration estimate
value_confidence: high
```

## Context

MICROSCOPE compares the accelerations of concentric titanium and platinum test masses in the same terrestrial field. The satellite rotation modulates the differential signal. The proposed Z/A charge prescription and the measured acceleration difference are distinct: the quoted result is the acceleration readout, not a fitted strength of that prescription.
