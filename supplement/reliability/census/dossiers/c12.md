## Context

Bottle experiments count surviving stored neutrons, while beam experiments count decay protons relative to neutron flux. The two methods have distinct systematic uncertainties and a known tension. The quoted PDG combined value is identified separately from method-specific results; the width ratio uses ℏ rather than h.
