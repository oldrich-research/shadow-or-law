## Context

The quoted fit is Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing in flat ΛCDM. Matter includes baryons, cold dark matter and the adopted neutrino contribution. Radiation and matter dilute differently as the universe expands, so equality refers to the redshift at which their densities coincide.
