## Context

The conditional normal-ordering evaluation uses Δm²21=7.41e-5 eV² and Δm²32=2.437e-3 eV² from PDG Table 14.7, first column. Oscillation experiments measure these differences, not the lightest mass. The expression for m1 is a supplied additional premise; Planck plus BAO constrains the sum independently within flat ΛCDM with three active neutrinos.
