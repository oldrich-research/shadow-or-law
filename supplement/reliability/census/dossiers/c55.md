```text
sigma: no Gaussian sigma supplied for this confidence interval
source: Particle Data Group 2024, lepton summary table; https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/tables/rpp2024-sum-leptons.pdf; electron, muon and tau entries; algebraic combinations use the quoted pole masses

```

## Context

Tau lifetime is too short for a storage-ring spin-precession measurement. Production cross sections and angular distributions constrain its magnetic interaction. The quoted PDG interval is a bound, not a central experimental determination with a symmetric standard deviation.
