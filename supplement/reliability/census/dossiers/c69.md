# c69 — Two-point normalization ratios R_T(5) and R_J(5)

```text
name: Two-point normalization ratios R_T(5) and R_J(5)
definition: Ratios of positive deficit coefficients c_T,c_J to η_1 in the large-N critical O(N) vector expansion: C_X/C_X,free=1-c_X/N+O(N^-2), η=η_1/N+O(N^-2); d=5.
dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: R_T(5)=c_T/η_1=22/105; R_J(5)=c_J/η_1=16/15
sigma: zero for the leading-order analytic ratios; higher orders in 1/N omitted
source: A. Petkou (1995), Physics Letters B 359 101; Alday, Henriksson and van Loon (2020), JHEP 01 063; https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9506116 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.02445; Petkou Eqs. 19,22,23, continued to d=5; second paper Eq. 2.50 checks the stress-tensor expression
value_confidence: high
```

## Context

η denotes the exponent in the scalar two-point decay |x|^(-(d-2+η)). Petkou uses a scalar scaling-dimension correction equal to η/2 and signed corrections C_X,1=-c_X. Evaluating his equations at d=5 and converting both signs and the factor of two gives the stated positive ratios; these are analytic expansion coefficients, not measurements of a finite-N specimen.
