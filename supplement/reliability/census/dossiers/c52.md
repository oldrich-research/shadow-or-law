## Context

Polarization B modes and large-angle microwave anisotropies constrain tensor fluctuations. The quoted bound combines Planck with BICEP2/Keck BK15 and BAO under the assumptions stated by Planck VI. It is an upper limit, not a measured tensor amplitude.
