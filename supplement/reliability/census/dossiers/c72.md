# c72 — CHSH Tsirelson bound

```text
name: CHSH Tsirelson bound
definition: Supremum of |<A0B0>+<A0B1>+<A1B0>-<A1B1>| for quantum measurements with ±1 outcomes.
dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: 2 sqrt(2)
sigma: zero for the analytic bound; experiment-specific error for a measured correlator
source: B. S. Cirelson (1980), Letters in Mathematical Physics 4 93–100; https://ma.huji.ac.il/~ohadfeld/Tsirelson/download/qbell80.pdf; original quantum correlation bound
value_confidence: high
```

## Context

Two separated measurement stations choose between two observables each. The four correlation estimates enter the CHSH sum. Quantum observables acting on different stations commute, while the two observables at a single station need not commute; a suitable entangled two-qubit preparation attains the bound.
