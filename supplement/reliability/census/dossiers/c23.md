## Context

Iterating a smooth unimodal map produces cycles with successively doubled periods. The limiting ratios apply to the stated quadratic-extremum class, not to every nonlinear map. The sign used for spatial rescaling can be absorbed in the orientation of its coordinate; the quoted magnitude follows the original article's abstract.
