## Context

The author-maintained GMTKN55 repository publishes reaction-level reference and PBEh-3c energies, along with their numerical deviations. These six subsets retain the specified molecular structures and energy differences. Sample dispersion describes variation among residuals, not uncertainty on a physical constant; PCONF21 has 18 available method entries in this release.
