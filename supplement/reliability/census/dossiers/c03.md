# c03 — Strong CP angle θ_bar

```text
name: Strong CP angle θ_bar
definition: Coefficient θ_bar=θ_QCD+arg det(M_q) of the CP-odd gluonic term after quark-mass phases are accounted for.
dimensions_units: angle; rad
value: |θ_bar| is constrained to approximately 1e-10 by electric-dipole searches, subject to hadronic conversion
sigma: no Gaussian sigma for this order-of-magnitude limit
source: Particle Data Group 2024, axion review; https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-axions.pdf; strong-CP angle and neutron electric-dipole discussion
value_confidence: med
```

## Context

A neutron electric dipole moment would be sensitive to this angle, alongside other possible CP-odd interactions. The conversion from a dipole limit to θ_bar uses a hadronic calculation. The dossier states the approximate constraint rather than a measured central angle.
