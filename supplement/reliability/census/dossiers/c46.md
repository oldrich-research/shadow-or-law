## Context

These entries combine Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing with BAO and Type Ia supernovae. The constant-w result and two-coefficient w(a) result are separate fits, not three independent estimates from one joint posterior. Expansion and distance measurements constrain the pressure contribution through the Friedmann equation.
