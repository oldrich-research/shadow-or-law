# c25 — CKM mixing angles and Cabibbo angle

```text
name: CKM mixing angles and Cabibbo angle
definition: Bundle θ12,θ13,θ23 and δ in the standard three-angle CKM matrix; λ=sin θ12.
dimensions_units: dimensionless sines; angle δ in rad
value: sin θ12=0.22501; sin θ13=0.003732; sin θ23=0.04183; δ=1.147
sigma: 0.00068; +0.000090/-0.000085; +0.00079/-0.00069; 0.026 respectively
source: Particle Data Group 2024, CKM review; https://pdg.lbl.gov/2024/reviews/rpp2024-rev-ckm-matrix.pdf; Eq. 12.28
value_confidence: high
```

## Context

Quark charged-current decays, neutral-meson mixing and CP asymmetries constrain the matrix entries. A global fit accounts for experimental and hadronic uncertainties. The listed sines and phase use PDG Eq. 12.28, not four separate uncorrelated experiments.
