## Context

The physical densities determine baryon loading, matter-radiation balance and clustering in the microwave spectrum. The quoted joint fit uses Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing in flat ΛCDM. The total includes the adopted neutrino mass contribution as well as baryons and cold dark matter.
