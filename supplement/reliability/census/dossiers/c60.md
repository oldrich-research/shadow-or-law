## Context

Photons diffuse through the baryon-electron plasma before decoupling, damping small-angle temperature and polarization fluctuations. Recombination inputs and fitted cosmological densities determine the diffusion length. The three displayed combinations use the same fit's r_drag=147.09 Mpc,r_*=144.43 Mpc and k_eq=0.010384 Mpc^-1.
