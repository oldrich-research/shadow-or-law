```text
name: Electron anomalous magnetic moment a_e

dimensions_units: dimensionless
value: 1.15965218046e-3
sigma: 1.8e-13
source: CODATA 2022 adjustment, NIST full numerical table; https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/Table/allascii.txt; named constants and standard uncertainties
value_confidence: high
```

## Context

Penning-trap measurements compare spin-precession and cyclotron frequencies. Precision QED predicts the anomaly as a series in α with smaller contributions from other interactions. The quoted value is the 2022 CODATA adjustment, not a discrepancy obtained by subtracting a selected theoretical estimate.
