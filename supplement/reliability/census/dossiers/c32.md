## Context

The running coefficient measures curvature of the logarithmic primordial scalar spectrum. Planck fits it together with amplitude and tilt rather than measuring it directly in one sky pixel. The quoted result allows one running coefficient and uses TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing.
