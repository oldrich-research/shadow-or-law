## Context

Storage-ring experiments compare spin-precession and orbital frequencies and calibrate the magnetic field with nuclear magnetic resonance. A theoretical prediction includes QED, weak and hadronic contributions. Different treatments of the hadronic terms produce different differences, so the reported experimental value is distinguished from that subtraction.
