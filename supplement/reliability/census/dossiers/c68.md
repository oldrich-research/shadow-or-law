## Context

Nuclear magnetic resonance and magnetic-moment frequency comparisons constrain these spin-one-half nuclei. The listed helion value refers to the unshielded nucleus, not a helium sample with electronic shielding. The two nuclei contain the same total nucleon count but different proton and neutron counts.
