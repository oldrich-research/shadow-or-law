These triples do not suggest a distinguished identification to me. A standard **constructed realization** exists for every row, however. Its adjustable parameters must not be mistaken for independently predicted constants.

**Common conventions.** Take a classical isotropic harmonic oscillator with 22 modes,
\[
H=\frac{\omega}{2}\sum_{j=1}^{22}(p_j^2+q_j^2),
\qquad d\mu=\frac{d^{22}p\,d^{22}q}{(2\pi)^{22}}.
\]
Coordinates and energies are dimensionless. Here \(x\) is **energy**, not inverse temperature. Integrating angular variables and then a simplex gives
\[
\rho(x)=\int\delta(x-H)\,d\mu
=\frac{x^{21}}{21!\,\omega^{22}}.
\]
The unnormalized canonical energy weight is
\[
\rho(x)e^{-\beta x}.
\]
Thus \(Z(\beta)=\int \rho(x)e^{-\beta x}\,dx\). For negative \(\beta\), restrict phase space to \(H\leq1\); the formulas then hold exactly for \(0<x<1\). This cutoff is **chosen to fit**, not inferred. Without it, the negative-temperature partition function diverges.

The mode count, frequency, and temperature below are all **chosen to fit**. These accounts establish mathematical realizability, not a special identification.
